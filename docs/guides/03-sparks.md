## What Is a Spark?

A **Spark** is a lightweight, zero-friction capture unit—similar to a scratchpad card or a private tweet to yourself. Use Sparks whenever you want to record something immediately without deciding where it belongs first.

Common uses for Sparks:
- A sudden architectural idea while debugging another ticket
- A screenshot of an error trace, benchmark chart, or UI inspiration
- A link or command snippet you want to investigate later
- A rough todo item that hasn't been scoped into a project yet

---

## Capturing a Spark

You can capture a Spark from anywhere inside the authenticated app:

1. **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press **`Cmd + K`** (macOS) or **`Ctrl + K`** (Windows/Linux) on any page.
2. **Floating Button**: Click the floating **Spark** pill in the bottom-right corner on desktop, or the center **Spark** button in the mobile bottom navigation bar.
3. **Sparks Feed**: Go to [Sparks](/sparks) and click **New Spark**.

### Attaching Images & Screenshots
Inside the Spark dialog, you can:
- **Paste directly (`Cmd/Ctrl + V`)** with an image on your clipboard.
- **Drag and drop** an image file (`PNG`, `JPEG`, `WEBP`, `GIF`).
- Click the upload area to browse from disk.

## Triaging & Promoting Sparks

Sparks are meant to be triaged rather than hoarded forever. From the [Sparks](/sparks) page, every card gives you fast actions to graduate the idea into structured work:

### Promote to a Project Item
Turn a Spark into an actionable backlog item inside any of your projects:
1. Click **Promote to Project** on the Spark card.
2. Select the target **Workspace** and **Project**.
3. Choose the item kind (`feat`, `fix`, `perf`, `refactor`, `spike`, `docs`, `chore`) and initial status (`Backlog`, `In Progress`, or `Shipped`).

### Convert to a Note
If a Spark grows into a deeper technical investigation or TIL:
1. Edit or copy the Spark content into a new [Note](/notes/new).
2. Expand it with full Markdown, code blocks, and reference sources.

### Filter & Search
Use the search bar on the [Sparks](/sparks) page to filter your captured thoughts by keyword, or clean up old sparks once they have been promoted.
