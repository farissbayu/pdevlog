## Your Autonomous Staff Engineer Mirror

Most engineers only get career feedback once a year—often from managers who haven't read their code. **[Profile & CV](/profile)** combines your **Resume/CV** with your live **Brag Logs** and **Notes** to give you an objective, evidence-backed read on your engineering trajectory.

---

## The Three Profile Tabs

### 1. CV & Resume (`/profile?tab=cv`)
- **Upload a PDF CV**: Drag and drop your resume PDF. Personal Dev Log parses the PDF in the background, converts it into clean structured Markdown, and automatically extracts your engineering persona.
- **Live Markdown Editor**: Prefer editing directly? Switch between **Preview** and **Edit** tabs to keep a canonical Markdown version of your CV right inside the app.

### 2. Engineering Persona (`/profile?tab=persona`)
- **Headline & Bio**: Your concise positioning statement and background summary.
- **Primary Stack**: Tags representing your core technologies (e.g., `TypeScript`, `React`, `Cloudflare Workers`, `PostgreSQL`).
- **Extract from CV**: Click **Extract Persona from CV** anytime to auto-populate your headline, bio, and stack from your latest CV Markdown.

### 3. Competency Analysis (`/profile?tab=skills`)
Click **Analyze Skills** to run the AI Staff Engineer audit across your CV, Brag Logs, and Notes. The audit produces:

- **Engineering Archetype** — Your current high-level technical identity (e.g., *Product-Centric Fullstack Craftsman*, *Systems & Reliability Builder*).
- **Executive Summary** — A candid synthesis of where you stand today based on real shipped evidence rather than buzzwords.
- **Core Superpowers** — Your strongest technical domains, each backed by concrete evidence from your CV and Brag Logs.
- **Growth Opportunities & Next Steps** — Honest blindspots holding you back from the next level, paired with actionable next steps—a project to build or a note to write—to close them.

---

## Dashboard Competency Snapshot

Once you run your first Competency Analysis, your [Dashboard](/dashboard) automatically displays a compact **Competency Snapshot** card showing your Archetype, top 3 Superpowers, and top 2 Growth Focus areas—keeping your monthly technical goals visible every time you open the app.
