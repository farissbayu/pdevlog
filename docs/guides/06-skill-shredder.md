## What Is Skill Shredder?

Traditional tutorials trap you in 20+ hours of passive video watching or boilerplate setup (CSS tweaks, user auth, folder bikeshedding) before you ever touch the core technology.

**[Skill Shredder](/skill-shredder)** acts like a Staff Engineer sitting next to you, stripping a new topic down to the bare minimum you need to run and verify in a single 60-minute session.

---

## Anatomy of a Shred

When you start a shred (either from a [Note](/notes) or directly in [Skill Shredder](/skill-shredder)), the AI generates four focused sections:

### 1. Fundamental Pillars
Boils the technology down to **2–3 core mental models**—why it exists, what problem it solves under the hood, and how data flows through it.

### 2. Spike 0 (15-Minute Proof of Life)
A concrete, copy-pasteable minimal terminal or script experiment designed to run in **under 15 minutes**. No UI frameworks, no databases unless required—just proving the core primitive works on your machine.

### 3. The Ignore List
An explicit list of sub-topics, edge cases, and production concerns you are **strictly forbidden** from worrying about during your first session. This prevents scope creep from derailing your learning.

### 4. Practice Challenge
A small, self-contained modification challenge you must implement **without AI autocomplete** to prove you actually understood the Fundamental Pillars.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. **Create a Note or Topic**: Write a short note on what you want to learn (or open an existing note) and trigger **Shred This Skill**—or manage your active sessions from [Skill Shredder](/skill-shredder).
2. **Check Off Steps**: As you read the pillars, run Spike 0, and complete the challenge, check off each step in the shred detail view to track your progress (`In Progress` → `Completed`).
3. **Export to TIL Note**: Once you finish the challenge, export your findings and working code snippets into a permanent [Note](/notes) so the knowledge compounds in your vault.
